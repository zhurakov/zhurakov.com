# Dao Ho Koi Pond notice

The files under `js/koi/src/` and their generated browser modules in `js/koi/dist/` originate from the public [`Dao-Ho/portfolio`](https://github.com/Dao-Ho/portfolio) repository. They are used here to provide the koi pond animation on the Pond page.

The small `js/pond-page.js` module is a local, framework-free adapter. Analytics from the upstream pond component are not included.
