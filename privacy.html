Privacy

Last updated: August 2, 2026.

Optional analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 only after you choose “Allow analytics.” Google Analytics is not downloaded and no analytics request is sent before that choice.

What is measured

When analytics is allowed, Google Analytics may process page views, navigation events, referrer information, and browser or device details. It may store first-party cookies whose names begin with _ga.

What stays disabled

Advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization are disabled. Analytics is used to understand how visitors use the site and to improve its content and navigation.

Your choice

Your choice is stored in your browser under hello-world:analytics-consent. If you clear this site's data in your browser, the site will ask for your choice again on your next visit.

Site operator and contact

This site is operated by Nikita Zhurakov. For privacy questions or requests, please contact me through GitHub.

Google Analytics and retention

Google provides the Analytics service and may process data in countries where it or its service providers operate. Analytics data is retained according to the retention setting of this site's Google Analytics property. Google’s handling of data is described in its Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, or to object to its processing. You may contact the site operator about these or other privacy requests.

Google documentation

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Analytics Consent Mode documentation.
